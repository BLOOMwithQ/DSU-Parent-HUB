Parent and Caregiver FAQ

Camp Dates, Time, and Daily Location
  • Camp dates: Monday, July 13 through Friday, July 24.
  • Camp time: 8:45 AM to 3:45 PM each day.
  • Morning location: Longwood Auditorium inside the Bank of America Building.
  • Address: Delaware State University, Bank of America Building, 1200 N. DuPont Highway, Dover, DE 19901.
Parent Drop-Off, Pick-Up, and Campus Directions
  • Drop-off: Please arrive between 8:45 AM and 9:00 AM.
  • Pick-up: Please pick up between 3:45 PM and 4:00 PM.
  • Best campus entrance: Enter from College Road rather than the main gate.
  1. Pass the Delaware State University main gate.
  2. Stay in the farthest right-hand lane.
  3. At the second traffic light, bear right onto College Road.
  4. When the black gate ends, turn into the parking lot at Campus Gate 2.
  5. Park in an undesignated spot closest to the building with the green dome.
  6. Walk toward the building with the green dome. This is the Bank of America Building.
  7. Students will begin the day in Longwood Auditorium.
Bus Transportation
  • Bus schedules are included in this parent hub.
  • Students should arrive 10 minutes before the listed bus stop time.
  • All buses leave DSU at 3:45 PM.
  • Times are estimated. Traffic and other conditions may affect exact times.
Meals, Snacks, and Dietary Options
  • Students will receive continental breakfast, lunch, bottled water, and snacks.
  • Students will eat in the Delaware State University cafeteria on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.
  • Students will participate in camp picnics on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
  • Non-meat and vegetarian options will be available.
Technology and Personal Devices
  • All technology equipment and supports will be provided to students at no cost.
  • Students may bring personal devices.
  • The STEM Achievers Program and Delaware State University are not responsible for lost or stolen electronic devices.
Student Expectations and Behavior
  • Students are expected to demonstrate respectful and appropriate behavior each day.
  • Bullying and inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated.
  • Discipline may include a verbal warning, one-on-one discussion, parent or guardian contact, and possible removal from the program.
  • Students who do not follow program expectations may be dismissed from camp.
Attendance and Roster Updates
  • The STEM Achievers Program is designed as a full two-week experience.
  • Consistent attendance helps students receive the full academic, STEM, and career exposure intended through the program.
  • If your scholar is no longer attending, please email camp staff as soon as possible.
Contact and Remind App
  • Remind: Text @6kh6cd2 to 81010, or join through the Remind app. Families will be added after administrator approval.
  • Email: vkhopkins@desu.edu and qmuhammad@desu.edu.
  • Please use email if your scholar is no longer attending or if you have questions.
